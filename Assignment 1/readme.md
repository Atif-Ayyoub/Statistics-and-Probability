Statistics and Probability Theory – MATLAB Assignment
📚 About This Repository
This repository contains MATLAB solutions for Assignment No. 01 of Statistics and Probability Theory. It covers basic statistical concepts, data analysis, frequency distributions, sampling techniques, and graphical representation using MATLAB.
🎯 Assignment Questions
Q1: Types of Variables, Measurement Scales and Measurement Error
Classification of qualitative and quantitative variables.
Measurement scales: nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio.
Calculation of measurement errors and mean absolute measurement error.
Data visualization using MATLAB.
Q2: Data Collection and Sampling
Simple random sampling.
Stratified sampling.
Systematic sampling.
Proportional sample size calculation and random student selection.
Q3: Frequency Distribution of a Discrete Variable
Frequency distribution of software defects.
Relative and cumulative frequency calculations.
Identification of mode.
Bar chart visualization.
Q4: Frequency Distribution of a Continuous Variable
Sturges' rule for determining class intervals.
Grouped frequency distribution.
Relative and cumulative frequencies.
Histogram and cumulative frequency curve (ogive).
Q5: Types of Frequency Curves and Data Presentation
Grouped frequency distribution of test scores.
Histogram.
Frequency polygon.
Cumulative frequency curve.
Q6: Stem-and-Leaf Display and Central Tendency
Stem-and-leaf display.
Arithmetic mean, median, and mode.
Boxplot or histogram visualization.
Analysis of distribution and skewness.
Q7: Arithmetic Mean, Weighted Mean and Median
Simple arithmetic mean.
Weighted mean.
Median and mode of coding-test scores.
Bar chart of course marks.
